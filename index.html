Strategy & Rules

The most dangerous failure mode is not failing at business. It is spending four years preparing to start.

Rule 01 — One active business

Explore freely, but only one business receives serious founder time at once.

Rule 02 — Sell before building

Whenever possible, manually deliver the outcome before building software.

Rule 03 — Customers beat ideas

A painful problem with existing spending is more valuable than a clever idea.

Rule 04 — Projects beat tutorials

Every major learning track should eventually produce something tangible.

Rule 05 — Certifications are tools

Never collect certificates simply to increase the number of certificates.

Rule 06 — Deep tech takes years

Do not rush semiconductor or advanced security specialization.

📜 Certification Strategy

Certifications are deliberately limited. They should unlock opportunities, not become the primary measure of progress.

Year Target Why Priority
Year 1 Optional foundation credential Only if useful for internships or structured learning. Low
Year 2 Security+ or equivalent Useful if cybersecurity becomes a serious specialization. Medium
Year 3 Cloud/security credential Only if justified by career direction or internship. Optional
Year 4 No automatic certification Prioritize portfolio, internships, research and business. Low
Certification test: Before studying for a certification ask:
  • Does it appear in jobs I want?
  • Does it help me obtain an internship?
  • Does it structure knowledge I actually need?
  • Could I demonstrate the same skill through a project?
